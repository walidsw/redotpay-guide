Step by step

Take it slowly. The whole thing is done inside the app.

  1. Download the app

    Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play (Android) and search RedotPay. Install it only from the official store, because fake copies exist.

    On iPhone, RedotPay says the ID check works best on iOS 12.2 or newer.

  2. Add the referral code, then sign up

    • On the log-in screen, tap Referral Code (Optional).
    • Paste the code: 4rt2t
    • Sign up with your email, Google account or Apple ID.
    • Link your phone number and email when the app asks.

    Code first. RedotPay says a referral code can only be added during registration, not after.

  3. Verify your identity (KYC)

    Open identity verification in the app and follow the screens:

    • Enter your country, name and date of birth.
    • Photograph your original ID, using one of the types shown on screen. It must be valid for 3+ more months. No photocopies or screenshots.
    • Do a quick face scan in good light, plain background, no hat, glasses or mask.
    • Check the details the app read from your ID.
    • Add your home address. No postal code? RedotPay says to type 000000.
    • Answer a short questionnaire: 4–5 questions, about 3 minutes.

    Review usually takes a few minutes. If they ask for extra documents (like a personal statement or source of funds), it can take 1–3 business days, and you have about 14 days to send them.

    You must be 18 or older, and each person can have only one verified account.

  4. Add funds by depositing crypto

    The card fee comes out of your RedotPay balance, so add funds before ordering a card.

    • Tap Deposit and choose a coin, e.g. USDT.
    • Choose the network, e.g. USDT-TRC20.
    • Copy the address (or scan the QR) and send from your own wallet or exchange on the same network.
    • Track it under Assets.

    Wrong network means lost money. Double-check the coin and network, and send a small test amount first. There's also a “Buy crypto” option with a bank card, but it may not work in every country.

  5. Get your card

    • Tap Card.
    • Virtual card: tap “Apply for card • 10 USD”. Works online and in mobile wallets.
    • Physical card: choose Physical card → “Apply for card • 100 USD” and add a shipping address.
    • Write the billing address in English, then tap Pay.

    The card fee is one-time and non-refundable, and the name on the card can't be changed later. Not every country can get a card, so check RedotPay's card restriction list first.

  6. Top up and start spending

    Keep crypto in your RedotPay balance. When you pay with the card, it's converted at that moment. Add more anytime by repeating step 4.

// common questions

FAQ

Is the referral code required?

No, it's optional. But it can only be entered at sign-up, so if you'd like to use mine, add it before you create your account.

What do I get for signing up?

RedotPay has offered new users a 5 USD sign-up reward. It's for spending only, can't pay the card fee, and lasts 30 days. Offers change, so check the app for the current one.

What does the person who shared the code get?

RedotPay pays the inviter a commission (a percentage of card fees and card spending) once you sign up and finish identity verification. Nothing is taken from your balance.

Is RedotPay available in my country?

RedotPay publishes lists of blocked countries for the app and for cards (for example the United States, Mainland China and Russia). The lists change, so check the help center.

Which ID can I use?

Only the document types the app shows for your country. It must be the original physical ID, valid for at least 3 more months.

Can I deposit taka or dollars from my bank?

No. RedotPay says fiat money can't be deposited or transferred to the card. You fund it with crypto.

Can I get the card fee refunded?

No. Once paid, the card fee can't be canceled or refunded, even if you delete the card.

Can I use it at an ATM?

Yes, with the physical card, at ATMs that support major card networks. The fee is 2% (see above), and the ATM may add its own.

Is my money safe?

Every crypto service carries risk: price swings, hacks, account freezes and rule changes. Only deposit what you can afford to lose, turn on every security option, and never share your password or codes with anyone, including people who say they're “support”.
